今日精讲

circle back　回头再议

语域：中性偏口语 · 会议

为什么值得细看

会议里用来结束一个暂时没有结论的话题，同时承诺会再处理它。它比 let's discuss later 更明确——带「我会回来找你」的意思。

容易用错

正式邮件里少用，显得随意；书面场合用 revisit 或 follow up on。

常用搭配

  • circle back on this
  • circle back next week
  • circle back after the audit

三个例句

  • Let's circle back on the tooling cost once we have the quote.　—　等报价回来我们再回头谈模具费。
  • I'll circle back with you after the supplier call.　—　跟供应商通完话我再回来找你。
  • We circled back twice and still landed in the same place.　—　我们回头议了两次，结论还是原地。

中式英语纠正

We must reduce the cost.
We need to take cost out.
take cost out 是制造业常用说法。
The quality is very good.
The quality is solid.
very good 太空泛，solid 表示稳定可靠。
Time is very tight.
We're up against a tight timeline.
后者把主语落在「我们」上，更像在汇报。
I will go to the factory tomorrow.
I'll be at the plant tomorrow.
汽车行业的工厂习惯叫 plant。
